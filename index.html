FAQ

Questions owners usually ask us.

What is Odoo, and is it right for an SME?

Odoo is a set of business apps (sales, inventory, accounting, HR and more) that all share one database. You start with the apps you need today and add more as you grow, so it suits small teams as well as larger companies.

How much does it cost?

There are two parts. The Odoo subscription is paid to Odoo, per user. Our implementation fee depends on the apps, users, customisation and data you bring over, and we give you a fixed scope and price after the consultation.

How long does implementation take?

Most SME projects go live within 8 weeks. Larger rollouts can go in phases, starting with the area that hurts most.

Can you move our data from SQL Account, AutoCount or Excel?

Yes. We migrate customers, suppliers, products, stock and opening balances, and can run both systems side by side during the switch so nothing is lost.

Is Odoo ready for LHDN e-Invoice (MyInvois)?

Yes. Odoo Enterprise has MyInvois integration built in, so invoices are submitted and validated straight from Odoo. We configure and test it with you before go-live.

Odoo Community or Enterprise?

Community is free but limited. Enterprise adds full accounting, e-Invoice, mobile apps and Odoo’s official support. We recommend whichever fits your needs and budget.

Do you provide training and support after go-live?

Yes. Your team is trained before go-live, and we stay on after launch to fix issues, train new staff and improve the system as your business grows.
